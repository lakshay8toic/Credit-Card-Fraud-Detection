Two decks, for two different jobs.

| **Credit Card Fraud Detection - Project Explanation.pptx** | 20 | Explaining the whole project. | **Credit Card Fraud Detection - Presentation.pptx** | 20 | An earlier viva deck over the same material, organised by topic rather than by unit. |
